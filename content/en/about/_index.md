## The short version

I'm a Software Engineer specialized in quality, with over 10 years in test automation and quality strategy, currently owning quality for AI features at [Miro](https://miro.com/) (100M+ users).

## Before that

I landed my first role as a software engineer at a small startup in Argentina while I was still at university. During that time, I unconsciously (or maybe not) focused mostly on quality, making sure every feature we shipped met our customer's expectations.

Six months later I joined [Claro](https://www.claro.com.ar/), one of the leading mobile companies in Latin America, where I got a taste of working inside a large corporation and learned how quality looks different at scale.

Right after that, I went back to the scale-up world, joining Tips, a healthcare software company working to expand their medical system across multiple hospitals in the country. There I had the privilege of working with some talented friends from university and took my first steps into mobile testing frameworks.

## Moving to the Netherlands

In 2018 I decided to move to the Netherlands. The idea was to take a sabbatical year and think about my next move. The following year I joined Eriks Digital, where I got my first experience building test automation frameworks and quality processes from the ground up, across very different projects (ERP systems, IoT and e-commerce).

About two years later, right as COVID-19 hit, I joined Miro during its hyper-growth phase. What started as a role focused on testing critical user journeys and third-party integrations grew, over time, into leading quality for entire teams, defining the standards that multiple engineering teams across the company rely on. This journey has been full of learnings, challenges, and some of the most exciting problems I could ever think to solve.

## Now
Recently, I've exploring in the area of back end engineering as well as AI evals for LLM based features in the domain of security and compliance.
Looking back, I think I've always been passionate about quality, understanding how well-built things work and what makes them robust and long-lasting, and I've carried that passion into my professional career.

If any part of this story resonates with you, or you just want to talk about quality, AI, or life as a SWE in a foreign country, feel free to reach out. I'd love to connect.
